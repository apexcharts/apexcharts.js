A trellis (small multiples) splits one dataset into a grid of panels: trellis.by: 'region' is the whole configuration. The trellis owns everything shared: the y domain (union of all panels, so panels never lie), the x window, one legend (click Target to hide it in every panel), one zoom/pan/reset toolbar, and the responsive column count (resize the window: 3 → 2 → 1 columns from minPanelWidth alone). The keyless Target series repeats in every panel as a reference line. Trellis is a premium feature; without a license it renders with a trial watermark.
