How does one region compare with the whole company?

Pick a region. The company's figure stays faded and the region's is drawn solid in front. Hover any arc or needle for both values.

Region

SumsThe region is a share of the company

The company's figure is the three regions added up, so a region's arc always ends inside it. On the rings, the solid arc is the region's contribution to each team's goal.

AveragesA region can beat the company

A score or a rate is an average, so a region can sit above the company. The satisfaction gauge uses indicator: 'lanes' to draw the two side by side; the needle gauge fades the company's needle.
