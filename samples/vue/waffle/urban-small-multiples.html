The century of the city

One mini-waffle per country: each square is 1% of its population living in a city.

Small multiples are the unit chart's grid layout in split mode: plotOptions.unit.grid.split: true draws one waffle per category, grid.total: 100 gives each tile 100 cells and grid.max: 100 fills value-of-100 of them, so every tile reads as a percentage against its faint trackColor backdrop. Switching the year is a plain chart.updateSeries(); each tile keeps its cells in place and only the fill boundary moves. The unit / waffle chart is a premium type; without a license it renders with a trial watermark.
