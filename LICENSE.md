ApexCharts is offered under a **dual-license model** to support individuals, startups, and commercial products of all sizes.

One license key covers every Apex product your plan includes, so you do not buy a separate key for each product.

For individuals, non-profits, educators, and small businesses whose **annual revenue, operating budget, funding, or equivalent financial resources are less than $2 million USD**.

✅ What's allowed:

- Personal, educational, or non-profit use
- Commercial use by small orgs (under $2M in annual revenue, budget or funding)
- Modifications, and sharing the source with attribution

🚫 Not allowed:

- Use by companies or entities at or above $2M in annual revenue, budget or funding
- Use in competing charting products
- Sublicensing under different terms

➡ By using ApexCharts under this license, you confirm that **you qualify as a Small Organization**.

### 💼 Commercial License (Paid)

The Commercial License applies to every paid plan: Community + Commercial, Pro, Premium and OEM.

The **Community License** is available at no cost **only** to organizations whose **annual revenue, operating budget, funding, or equivalent financial resources are under $2M USD**.
If you, your parent company, affiliates, or any entity under common control reach $2M USD or more on any of those measures, you must purchase one of our paid licenses.

Pro and Premium are priced per developer, and priced on the features you use rather than on your size.

✅ What's included:

- Use in internal tools, client work, and commercial applications
- Client work includes a system you build for a single client and hand over to run on the client's own servers
- Modifications, and deploying your application to its users
- 12-month subscription with updates, and technical support on Pro and above

🚫 Not allowed:

- Redistribution in toolkits, SDKs, or platforms
- Selling or licensing the same product to multiple customers, which is redistribution and needs the OEM License below
- Use by unlicensed developers
- Competing charting products

### 🔄 OEM / Redistribution License (Paid)

Required if you ship ApexCharts inside a **product you distribute** to customers or other developers, or inside a **hosted product where users build, customize, or configure charts**, such as:

- No-code dashboards
- Developer platforms
- Embedded BI tools
- SDKs

✅ What's included:

- Redistribution rights for **one application or product**
- **Every licensed feature**, at the highest tier
- 12-month subscription with updates & support

⚠️ **Developer licenses are purchased separately.** The OEM License grants redistribution rights and carries no developer seats. Each developer who works with ApexCharts in that application also needs a **Community + Commercial** license of their own, which is free for organizations under $2M in annual revenue, budget or funding. That is the tier whatever features your product uses, because the license key comes from the OEM License and already renders every licensed feature.

Your end users need no license of their own. A second application needs a second OEM license. White-label, resale, and affiliate arrangements are quoted separately.

✅ OEM **not required** for a hosted app that only shows predefined charts, which users **cannot** build, customize, or configure. Using features your developers enabled, including Premium features, does not by itself require an OEM license. OEM is for products whose purpose is letting users build charts or dashboards: dashboard and report builders, embedded BI, no-code tools, SDKs.

### ⏳ When a subscription ends

Your license key expires, and **licensed features render with a trial watermark**. You no longer receive updates, new features, or technical support. Work built only from free features is unaffected, and your existing deployment keeps running.

For OEM licenses, redistribution must stop until the license is reinstated. Cancelling or not renewing a subscription is not termination of the [Terms of Use](https://apexcharts.com/legal/terms/).
